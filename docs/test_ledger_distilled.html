What the chain is. Build a taxonomy on the train side only, route held-out questions to its cells, rank models exactly where a key exists, judge with one frozen recipe where it does not, and publish pair directions only at a stated calibration status. Every published number is checkable against ground truth, and the record refuses what it cannot support.

What the campaign found. The judge's accuracy comes from the reference answer and the verify-first mechanics, not from anything grown on the tree. The frontier order cannot be recovered by any judge, reference or budget. The tree earns its place through routing, question support and the split, and the geometry alone certifies more structure than the category labels impose.
