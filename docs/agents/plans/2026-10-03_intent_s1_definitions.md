**(a) Descent for EVAL (T-C8).** PROPOSED: a Python port of the production inference trickle, `TaxonomyService.routeQueryToLeaves` -> `TaxonomyOperations.routeQuery(isInference=true)` -> `TaxonomyTrickler.trickle` (L264-L425). (1) `updateAllShrinkages` first: r_p = ||sum_c w_c mu_c||, where w_c = child queryWeights mass, normalised (GraphNode L144-L190). (2) At each node, children are scored by **meanKappa x dot**. The code uses the mean of the sibling kappas, not each child's own kappa: copy the code, not its comment (L176-L191). x = the raw vector truncated to the child's sliceDim, then L2-normalised (`DataModels.projectTo` L193). (3) Gate: stop if max child dot < max(0, r_p - descentMargin) x parent dot. (4) Beam: keep children within routingBeamGamma of the best dot; log-softmax over the beam; mass pushed in topological order; nodes below ln(1e-30) pruned. (5) Floor and cap, as L384-L410: membershipFloor share over the candidates (internal nodes count as candidates because DAG_MAX turns residual routing on, TaxonomyConfig L404), then maxLeafAssignments. (6) Top-1 = the highest-mass LEAF that has TRAIN members (the `profiles` filter, Runner L1665). No leaf means UNROUTED, and UNROUTED counts as wrong (G11's `<unrouted>` rule). The four knobs (descentMargin, routingBeamGamma, membershipFloor, maxLeafAssignments: 0.12 / 0.2 / 0.25 / 5) are read from the snapshot's settings, never typed in as literals. The port refuses non-empty crossLinkChildIds. ACCEPTANCE (the g13_breakdown I3 bar): on GATE, the Python Top-1 must equal the engine's own per-query Top-1 in `<run>/validation/MAIN_routing_diagnostics.txt` (parsed as in d4_arrival.held_out_routes) on at least 99.9% of GATE texts; otherwise the port refuses. That check exercises the port on the build's own reserved items, and EVAL never enters the JVM. flatLeaf = argmax cos(x, leaf vmfMu) at the leaf sliceDim (anchor_free_readout.route; UnionRouting FLAT_LEAF). ALTERNATIVE: a hard greedy argmax-child descent. Why not: that is not the engine's rule, because it drops the gate and the beam. The stops the gate causes are exactly what made G11's D_fd large, so this alternative would mis-state T-C8. A second alternative is to put EVAL through the JVM in a separate routing-only pass: that breaks HYGIENE ("EVAL physically absent").

**(b) Modal leaf (T-C4).** PROPOSED: the modal leaf of intent i = the leaf that receives the most of i's TRAIN items under flatLeaf, counted per item (B2 multiplicity). Ties go to the smallest node id. This is the assignment T-C1 / T-C1b already use (score_cells' TRAIN-majority rule). LCA distance = the number of edges from leaf a to the LCA plus from leaf b to the LCA. Parents are taken by first discovery over childIds (UnionRouting.treeParents; this equals treeParentId on a tree). The multi-parent count is printed. Distance is 0 when two intents share a leaf. Arms: M-AF and C-AF. Sign: r = Pearson(symmetric D3 pair count, -distance), with a one-sided permutation p (the registration leaves the sign implicit; r 0.35 is predicted positive). ALTERNATIVES: (i) snapshot membership weighted by queryWeights. Why not: soft membership (up to 4 leaves per text at floor 0.25) gives a leaf assignment that differs from T-C1's. (ii) EVAL items. Why not: D3's confusion is measured on EVAL, so both Mantel matrices would share one sample.
