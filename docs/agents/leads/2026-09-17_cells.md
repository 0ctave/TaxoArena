## 1. What the record says a cell must be

### 1.1 Two kinds of cell exist in the record, and they are not the same object
- A CERTIFIED SITE is a split site whose observed separation clears its own 300-rep within-node site null at p95
  (H9b: production statistic; P2: 6/66 on the frozen tree, trunk = 5 depth-1 splits; bareq512_s42 first site null
  2026-09-17: 10 of 70, experiment_results/promoted/site_null_bareq512_s42.csv, CAP-GATE-POSTHOC). It is a GEOMETRIC
  certificate: "certification is a published certificate per node, never an admission gate" (FRAMEWORK Stage 2 L53;
  ADAPT: "certify by pruning, not gating").
- A FUNCTIONAL FRINGE leaf is a leaf accepted by the z-gate (dJ >= 2 SE_dJ, ascending-k first accept) that does NOT clear
  its null. On the anchor-free tree of record that is 53 of 69 sites (ANCHOR-FREE-CERT: "a certified trunk plus a
  functional fringe"); on the anchored o0 control 60 of 65 (ANCHOR-FREE). The fringe is what the router uses and what
  every leaf-level readout is scored on.
- The flat bar 0.025 sits BELOW the whole within-node p95 band 0.029–0.075 (H9b); "behavioural validation is
  load-bearing" (distilled). The bar is a proposal filter, not a certificate.

What this means for cells as capability objects:
(a) "Certified" and "carries a model-performance contrast" are different predicates with 29% overlap; on the promoted

(b) The capability content that REPLICATES out of sample lives in the trunk (root + 2 depth-1 + one more), i.e. at 2–5
