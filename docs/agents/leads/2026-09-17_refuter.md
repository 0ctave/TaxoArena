## 1. The goal taken apart, claim by claim

### 1.1 "capability"

**Strong wording.** The framework measures what a model can do.

**What the record has already shown cannot be delivered.**
- Every published number is verification fidelity against a key, a checker, or a possibly wrong reference; there is no human preference or ground-truth-of-capability layer anywhere (CONTEXT §1; distilled L61; FRAMEWORK L251).
- Where the judge is the instrument, the verdict is largely a transcription of the reference: match-level KEY +0.788 ± 0.069 vs REF +2.177 ± 0.072, ratio 2.76 on x12 (n 9,142), 2.68 on the ensemble arm (L1294-1296); qualified by FORMAT-REF to an upper bound, clean causal REF +1.811, ratio 2.0-2.8 (L1297-1300). My own recompute: when the reference sides with the key-wrong model the judge is right 0.104 of the time (section 0). "Agreeing with the reference beats being right" is the deployed judge's operating characteristic, not a corner case.
- The reference's correctness is itself a per-cell quantity (REF-BY-CELL 0.727-0.952, FRAMEWORK L191), so a judge-built (model x cell) row inherits the reference's cell profile. TOPIC-DIFFICULTY makes this explicit inside a cell: accuracy A falls 15.5pp [-18.5, -12.4] easy → hard, but the reference-correct rate falls 98.4% → 77.7% and holding it fixed the drop is -4.3pp; 72-79% of the difficulty contrast is the reference's; the judge's own residual is -4.5pp [-6.7, -2.5] (L5135-5140, L5193-5194).
- A perfect reference does not turn the judge into a capability meter: ORACLE 0.612 → 0.777 (+16.4pp, 55:6, p 5.4e-11) with a residual 0.223 that is the judge's own; 49 of 67 residual errors are dual-order abstentions and 16 of 18 committed errors go to the longer answer (L1329-1354). AGGREGATE stacks tie resolution to 0.857 and the arx/iask order is still at chance (win 0.496-0.533, p 0.29-0.95; L1531-1555).
- Length is causal: truncating the correct answer drops correct verdicts 0.766 → 0.259 (183:1) and no instruction removes it (distilled L131). On both-right pairs (66% of the easy tercile) the judge takes the longer answer at +3.44 / 1k chars (L5144-5146). FRONTIER: 73% of the "invented" winners on key-tied frontier pairs went to the longer answer (L863-870).
- Reference-agreement leakage inflates whichever contestant mirrors the reference: DECOMP composition +0.116 [+0.092, +0.142] = 148% of the ARENA-13 gap, cell-matched residual 6:10 p 0.45 (L1267-1271). A newcomer that happens to answer like the reference gains for free.

**What it delivers under weaker wording.**
- On keyed cells: a per-(model, anchor) **accuracy** table with a question-bootstrap SE, pair directions by exact McNemar, key-built at 0 judge calls (FRAMEWORK L9, L115). That is a benchmark table; the judge and the tree add nothing to it (ORACLE-BOARD rho 1.000; key profile R 0.9135 vs judge 0.6728-0.7183). The taxonomy's only contribution to it is routing, which **attenuates** every (model x anchor) contrast by ~20% at 19% misrouting and 30-35% in the smallest anchors (ROUTING-ATTENUATION, FRAMEWORK L53, L183; bareq512 misfiles 24.7% of the pool).
- On unkeyed cells: "verification fidelity against a possibly wrong reference under STACK-v2", a possibly wrong-reference agreement score with a length bias, honest only when the reference state and the cell's reference accuracy are printed beside it.
- On checker cells with terminated traces: 0.958 / 0.993 key-decidable (STAGE3-MATH-CAPFREE L3996-3997) — the one place the judge's fidelity approaches the key — at the cost of excluding 16.5% of pairs (cap-hit).

**Evidence needed for the strong wording.** (a) An independent capability criterion that is not the key the reference was graded against: none exists in the programme and MMLU-Pro cannot supply one. (b) A reference whose correctness does not co-vary with the contestants' (REF-QUAL gate (3) is accuracy-coupled: agreement and accuracy correlate 0.982 across the roster, L1307; REF-QUAL-GATE3-COND UNDETERMINED, FRAMEWORK L41). (c) A judge whose error is not length-shaped when the reference is right (ORACLE: 89% of committed errors to the longer answer). None of (a)-(c) is on the record or registered.

### 1.2 "profile"

**Strong wording.** A per-cell capability profile of a model with intervals, at the granularity the taxonomy provides.

**What the record has already shown cannot be delivered.**
- Sub-anchor rows: refused on three grounds and closed. PROFILE-TREE-ROBUST corrected: with a within-anchor null the key's +0.8143 sits at the null median +0.8130 (95th +0.8321); (model, anchor)-demeaned cross-tree r on the most-changed third is -0.0711 key / -0.0841 judge (L3248-3263). KEY-SUBANCHOR-SCALE on the full keyed set (11,205 q, train side included, the most favourable case): on questions whose cell actually changes r = +0.006 (containment < 0.5, n 1,910) to -0.067 (containment < 0.25, n 1,239); reaching 0.70 would need ~3e17 q/leaf (L3441-3443). Judge leaf reliability R 0.09-0.36 across instruments (FRAMEWORK L156). CONTEXT §5 lists sub-anchor profiles as closed.
- The anchor profile is not the tree's: the anchors are the 14 pinned MMLU-Pro categories, shared by two trees on 2,800 of 2,811 questions "by fiat" (L2556-2557; review item 2). So the "profile the taxonomy gives" is the corpus's category labels. ANCHOR-FREE shows the geometry certifies more splits (16/69 vs 5/65) but its depth-1 layer has 2 cells at purity 0.188 and an undefined key-profile R (L4316, L4325); it is a structural artefact "not arena-bound for boards" (CONTEXT §3) and one draw on a 70.4%-in-sample split (L4628-4631; ANCHOR-FREE-REDRAW registered, unrun). - Judge-built anchor reliability is one point estimate on the bar: x12 0.6728 [0.4932, 0.8524] FAIL; merged 0.7183 [0.6076, 0.8291], PASS-WITH-INTERVAL, resting on Physics and Math (dropping either takes it below 0.70), margin one halving-SD (L3457, L3462; FRAMEWORK L117). Per-anchor certifiability has never been measured; History (119 q), Health (129), Philosophy (132) carry "unmeasured" (FRAMEWORK L160). Union strata (52 cells) have no reliability measurement at all (FRAMEWORK L258).
- Stratum boards inside a cell: every difficulty-stratified board is UNDETERMINED (nominal < 3 in 42 of 42 anchor strata; 5,233 called, 11 established wrong) — a stratified reading certifies nothing the whole-cell board does not (L5163-5164, L5184).

So the top of every profile column is a band of 4-6 models with NOT RESOLVED rows (FRAMEWORK L121).
- "Certified" cells vs "functional" cells: the site null certifies 5 of 65 splits on the anchored tree and 6/66 on the frozen tree, the trunk is 5 depth-1 splits imposed by labels (distilled L82-83); certification is "a published certificate per node, never an admission gate" (FRAMEWORK L53). Full profile coverage is ~3,100 cce per newcomer at the x20 density (FRAMEWORK L212), and the x20 density's own profile contribution was "more verdicts" +0.093 net of "more models" -0.047, total +0.0455 [-0.0442, +0.1352], not distinguishable from zero (L3458).

**What it delivers under weaker wording.** A 14-row (model x MMLU-Pro category) table: key-built where keyed (R 0.9135; SE per cell; McNemar directions), judge-built otherwise (theta/SD_cell, clustered SE, R 0.72 [0.61, 0.83] at the board level only); a certified partial order per cell (150 of 190 ORDERED pooled, 0 wrong, at 0.975 with "calibration UNDETERMINED") and a per-model band (certified above, below, unseparated), e.g. gemini (0, 14, 5), gpt-4o-mini (3, 10, 6), Llama-2-7b (18, 0, 1) (FRAMEWORK L119). Sub-anchor, per-question, within-frontier and stratum rows are refused at the API (FRAMEWORK L113).

**Evidence needed for the strong wording.** A per-cell (not board-level) reliability with a question-bootstrap interval for each of the 14 anchors and each of the 52 union strata (PROFILE-INTERVAL-ALL, registered item 9, unrun); a tree whose cells below the anchor survive a change of tree on re-celled questions (the record says no tree does, and no cell size fixes it); a frontier order (the record refuses it by rule).

### 1.3 "continually"

**Strong wording.** The framework re-judges over time, detects drift, and its numbers remain comparable across epochs, judges and providers.

**What the record has already shown cannot be delivered (or has not yet been shown at all).**
- The canary has no measured floor: pass 1 (600 calls, 300 matches, 0 invalid) has "NO OUTCOME YET"; the floor is read at pass 2, refused before 2026-09-22 (L3628-3629; distilled L191 "that the deployment has not drifted" is a refused claim). Design MDE: a single epoch of 300 has MDE ~2.7pp and power ~0.59 against a 3pp drift; ~1.5pp needs three pooled epochs (FRAMEWORK L141). DECOMP's 5.7% is a contested-frontier floor and is not the null for the mixed set (L141, L245).
- The canary is blind to reference drift: the reference cache carries no model id, prompt hash or fingerprint (FRAMEWORK L37, L245); arm B (re-mint 200 questions per quarter, alarm at 0.89 against REF-SC's 94% self-agreement) is DESIGN, unrun.
- Provider nondeterminism shows up as a denominator effect: JUDGE-WIRING on identical bytes, convention B Kotlin 0.888 (n 260) vs Python 0.914 (n 256), delta -2.6pp [-4.7, -0.6], carried by which matches each arm committed on, with 2 of 251 shared commitments discordant (L4159-4170); on the whole board the two draws agree inside 1.9pp (A +0.4pp [-0.2, +1.0], B +0.2pp [-0.2, +0.6]) but the established-label b1 identification sits on opposite sides of 3.84 (GROK-CALIB 4.47 vs Kotlin 2.26) on ~26 events (L4254-4267). "ORDERED-JUDGED available for grok" therefore rests on which draw one looked at; the framework's own text carries that bracket (L4266-4267).
- Test-retest noise is intrinsic: 5.7% on byte-identical prompts, 5.0% at temperature 0 (CI [-0.030, +0.017]); re-judging the same question adds no independent evidence, so continual re-judging cannot sharpen a profile, only new questions can (FRAMEWORK L153, L261; L2537-2539).
- Content-filter refusals are deterministic per question and permanent: 13 of 3,445 pool questions have no reference (3,432 cached, my count); 1 of 302 ORACLE, 1 of 800 newcomer matches, 1 of 9,123 Kotlin board (L1328, L3860-3861, L4241); rule 10 keeps them in the guard denominator forever (CONTEXT §4.10). Over time the judgeable set can only shrink.
- Re-judge cost: a judge change is "the most expensive update in the system": ~2,100 cce paired check plus ~20,000 cce calibration board above the 4,000-match rule (FRAMEWORK L193, L221); the Kotlin board of record cost 18,246 calls (L4235); a mixed-judge board is forbidden (L4164-4165). Continual operation with a judge that dies or changes price means re-buying the certificate.
- Widener parameters are roster-, judge- and corpus-specific; transfer UNDETERMINED in every direction (FRAMEWORK L254): BUDGET-TRANSFER 1,104 / 10(2) / 1.59; JUDGE-TRANSFER 354 / 0 / 0.93. So every epoch that adds a model or a corpus reopens the certificate's calibration status, which is UNDETERMINED on every board in reach and needs ~9 expected wrong calls (~6,500 keyed edges, ~7.3x x12 support) to become anything else (L253).
- Epoch guards that would make continual operation safe are DESIGN or FAIL: TRAIN-ONLY-TREE FAILS on every tree of record (2,437 / 37 / 427 exact EVAL texts among members; FRAMEWORK L229); EPOCH-NULL registered, unrun (L368); the FOREIGN gate is UNDETERMINED and not scorable as written (L53); optional stopping under adaptive allocation is unaddressed (L239).

**What it delivers under weaker wording.** A frozen recipe (STACK-v2, temperature omitted, cap 8192, dual order, TIE on flip) with a frozen 300-match canary set and one pass in the cache; an append-only verdict ledger with provenance (finish_reason, response id, batch_id) on every row since 2026-09-16; a resume path; and a stated cost per epoch (~19,000 cce, 3,700 judged matches under the cap, FRAMEWORK L223). "Continually" today means "repeatable", not "monitored".

**Evidence needed for the strong wording.** Canary pass 2 with a floor and an alarm that has been exercised; arm B on the reference vintage; a power statement beside b1 identification (or pooled labels across draws); a demonstrated epoch on the union with the FOREIGN gate scorable and EPOCH-NULL read; at least one judge or provider change survived with the certificate recomputed.

### 1.4 "agents"

**Strong wording.** The framework judges agents: multi-step trajectories, tool use, process quality.

**What the record has already shown cannot be delivered.**
- There is no trajectory, tool log or process-checker object anywhere in the record (my grep: 0 hits in the ledger, programme and framework). FRAMEWORK L269: "Nothing here judges agent traces; the registered >= +5pp verifier gain on trace-shaped tasks was never measured (GEN-ARENA had no no-verifier arm)."
- STACK-v2 is a single-answer verifier: two final-answer traces, one cached reference answer, verify-first mechanics, the option letter or checker value extracted at ingest (FRAMEWORK L39, L73). The only per-constraint object is IFEval's pass vector, the weakest cell in the programme: 0.658 with a specification reference, ties 25.6% (GEN-ARENA, FRAMEWORK L280); IFEval stays PREDICTIVE-ONLY (L360).

A long agent trajectory is the length-bias case at scale, and there is no measurement of it.
- Judge-family affinity to a contestant sharing the judge's vendor is untested since ARENA-13 (FRAMEWORK L260); for agents built on the judge's own model family this is the first question anyone would ask.

**What it delivers under weaker wording.** "Judges single-turn answers of LLMs on keyed, checker or reference-backed questions." The word "agent" is bridged in the thesis title as motivation-not-deliverable (memory: thesis-title-fixed) and the framework's own limits section says so.

**Evidence needed for the strong wording.** A trace-shaped corpus with a process checker or an outcome checker plus a no-verifier arm (the registered ≥ +5pp gain), a length-matched or truncation-controlled arm on trajectories, and an accuracy bar under both tie conventions on a keyed slice (GEN-ARENA (1): ≥ 0.75). None is registered.

## 2. The minimal honest object the framework can promise today

**One sentence.** For a corpus with a key or a checker, TaxoArena publishes a (model x anchor) verification-fidelity table over the corpus's own 14 pinned categories with question-bootstrap intervals, a certified partial order per anchor at 0.975 labelled "calibration UNDETERMINED", and a band (above / below / unseparated) per model, for the roster and the one frozen judge it was fitted on, with the frontier as one unordered band, nothing below the anchor, no preference or agent-process claim, and no drift guarantee until the 300-match canary's second pass lands.

Everything else in the goal sentence is either refused by the record (frontier order, sub-anchor rows, "calibrated", per-question ranks, agents), or unmeasured (drift, per-cell certifiability, union-strata reliability, cost axes).
