## What it delivers, and what it declines to deliver (one page)

It delivers, for every (model, question) on the eval side of a corpus, four recorded facts and nothing invented: the model's extracted answer; its correctness against the key or checker where one exists; every dual-order verdict the question received, with provenance; and the question's cell and reference. Everything published above the question is an aggregate of those facts with a stated cell, denominator, interval and publication class.

On keyed corpora (MMLU-Pro, BBH, GPQA, MuSR, MATH via checker) the board, the (model x anchor) profile and every pair direction come from the key, at zero judge calls: the key as verdict gives rho 1.000 and 0 violations on the x12 schedule (ORACLE-BOARD, UPHELD) and a key-built anchor profile has split-half reliability 0.9135 where the judge's is 0.6728 (PROFILE-RELIABILITY, UPHELD). Judge calls on keyed cells buy exactly two things: a calibration board for the judge's certificate and a canary. On unkeyed cells the judge is the instrument: STACK-v2 frozen (cheap judge, one cached reasoning reference per question, verify-first mechanics, dual order with TIE on flip), answer-disagreement-filtered schedule, question-clustered Bradley-Terry with an agreement-shaped systematic widener, publication at 0.975 with the calibration status printed beside every board. [P-FAM pointer 2026-09-27 (docs/reviews/RULING_w0_p2dca_family.md, 5effe96); long form at L3: judged numbers on this line drawn from the p2dca judged sample (x12 / R3 / ARENA-V2 / GROK-CALIB / Kotlin board, e.g. the judge's 0.6728) are p2dca judged family — design provenance (voice 2), not evidence; the key numbers (0.9135, the key-as-verdict rho) are not in the family]

It delivers per-cell tier maps by the record's own reading-down rule, a certified partial order (the ORDERED edge set), and per model a band (how many models are certified above it, below it, and unseparated from it). The router consumes the undominated set of a cell (models no other model is certified above) and picks within it on declared cost, measured latency or standardised strength. On the pooled 20-model board of record under widener + key-tie decline at 0.975 that set is six models (iask_pro, gemini-3.1-pro, claude-3-5-sonnet, arx_3, gpt-4o, arx_0314; recomputed from key_tie_decline's edge tables, 150 ORDERED of 190, 0 wrong, 0 cross-tier key inversions).
