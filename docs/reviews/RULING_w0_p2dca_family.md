## 2. Why one bracket, and why "history" rather than "cite with the bracket"

**The defects, with pointers:**
- **D1. Split.** p2dca is id-disjoint only from bareq512_s42: 37 exact / 70 norm-v1 EVAL texts among its members, 28 norm-only twins in its induction corpora (ledger L88 bracket; programme L14280-L14340). The frozen tree's split was p8a29, not p2dca (incident doc L4-L12).
- **D2. Schedule.** `experiment_configs/x12_crossdomain.toml` binds `snapshotId = "20260727_042523_Headless_Run_Auto_ge"` (L118), with "rubrics already in the snapshot" (L131).
  - The x12 schedule was cut on that tree's 87 cells ("all 87 cells", ledger L102 and L198). Its routing was in-sample for 2,437 of the 3,445 judgeable questions (incident doc L12; ledger L148 records 2,425).
  - R3 is the x12 re-judge sample and "the reuse sample for J1/J2/J3-R/RUBRIC-512/STACK" (ledger L103).
  - ARENA-V2, GROK-CALIB and the Kotlin board are the x12 MAIN schedule; x20 is its strict superset (ledger L295; CONTEXT L28).
- **D3. Rubric channel.** The r8-era cell rubrics were induced on corpora holding about 71% of the judged questions with the answer ticked (incident doc L10-L13). J1 and J1-R reuse the frozen-tree cell prompt (ledger L195). STACK-v2's anchor rubric is bareq512_s42's, so D3 reaches it only through D1's twins (ledger L186-L187).
- **D4. Selection.** STACK-v2 was the best of a family of recipe arms, all read on R3's 944 key-decidable pairs with no family-wise control. 0.865 is that sample's maximum (FW L79 bracket).
  - PAIR-NOREF's reference arm is the cached selected recipe, "byte-identical to stack_v2_promptv2_grok.db except the reference block is removed" (ledger L217). So even the paired contrast inherits the reference arm's selection.
- **D5. Termination.** 51 of 58 caches cannot distinguish NON_TERMINATED from wrong. "Every board of record (x12 MAIN, x20, R2, R3 and the whole rubric line) is in the 51" (ledger L263). Convention A levels therefore mix truncation with error. The rule "NON_TERMINATED is never a verdict" cannot be enforced on them.

**Why not A4's carve-out** (paired contrasts citable, levels not). I weighed it, and I agree that within-match contrasts are internally the most robust family results. I decline it as an EVIDENCE rule for four reasons:
- (i) D4 reaches the most-cited paired contrast (PAIR-NOREF) through its reference arm.
- (ii) Nearly every paired contrast runs on R3, a sample stratified on the frozen tree's cells (600 of 1,980 top-cluster matches). The contrast's size is a property of that weighting, not of MMLU-Pro.
- (iii) A robustness tier inside the bracket reopens the per-number triage that A4 (review H3) set out to replace with one ruling.
- (iv) Content-closed routes exist for every clause that would use it. RP04 gives the level by reference class at zero calls. RP20's optional ONE leg (REF-PAIRED-V2) is the only drafted content-closed PAIRED reference contrast.

The ranking A4 proposed is kept, as a ranking of planning priors (section 3.3).

**What this does not contradict.** The incident doc (L41) lists judge-behaviour results as "NOT affected" by the rubric channel. This ruling does not say they are contaminated. It rests on D2, D4 and D5, on the adopted boundary (A4), and on the existence of replacements. It does not rest on the rubric channel alone.

**Where the words come from.** The author said on 2026-09-26 that r8 "was flawed". That concerned r8. The extension to the post-incident STACK-v2 work is A4's widened boundary, which the author adopted on 2026-09-27, with the words left to the author (B7 item 6). The script premise "the p2dca judged family is HISTORY" is not treated as authority here. This ruling reaches the same conclusion on the reasons above.

**Correction to the plan's proposed words (A4).** "whose anchor rubrics were induced while 71% of the pool sat on that tree's train side" is true of the r8-era frozen-tree cell rubrics only. The STACK-v2 anchor rubric was induced on bareq512_s42 with p2dca withheld by id (ledger L87, L186). The long form above states both separately.
