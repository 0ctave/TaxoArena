\chapter{The Frozen Taxonomy}\label{app:frozen-tree}

Every arena result in this thesis is attributed to one construction artefact:
the taxonomy induced by \texttt{experiment\_configs/freeze\_mcs55.toml} at
seed~42, stored as snapshot \texttt{20260727\_042523} in \texttt{snapshots.db}.
The tree was frozen before any arena run reported here was launched and was not
modified afterwards. This appendix reproduces it, so that a reader can check any
per-cell claim against the actual partition rather than against a description of
it.

The tree has $154$ nodes: one root, $14$ anchors at depth~1, and $87$ leaves at
depths ranging from~2 to~6. Splitting below the anchors is almost entirely
binary --- $60$ of the $66$ internal splits produce two children, five produce
three, and one produces four --- so depth, not breadth, is what separates the
regions the arena scores. Leaves hold between $54$ and $396$ queries, with a
median of $88$.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{frozen_tree}
\caption{The frozen taxonomy as a horizontal icicle: anchors as rows, depth as
columns, row height proportional to routed query mass. Only the fourteen anchor
names are labelled; the leaves are named in Figure~\ref{fig:tree-full}. The
ragged right edge shows where a branch stopped splitting before the maximum
depth of~6.}
\label{fig:frozen-tree}
\end{figure}

Row heights in Figure~\ref{fig:frozen-tree} are query \emph{memberships}, which
are not the same as unique queries. The construction placed $8{,}299$ unique
queries, and that is the figure Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology} reports as the
placed corpus; summing the query lists of the $87$ leaves gives $9{,}141$. The
difference is retained multi-membership from construction, with $826$ queries in
two leaves and $8$ in three. It does not affect the arena results: secondary
leaf memberships were withdrawn before scoring, so a held-out query routed into
the tree contributes to exactly one cell. The overlap is a property of the
construction corpus, not of the evaluation.
