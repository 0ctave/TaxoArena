\section{The Paired-Run Roster (Run~B)}

Table~\ref{tab:model-roster} is the roster Run~B actually executed,
taken from the run manifest
(\texttt{experiment\_results/arena\_math\_paired/seed\_42/manifest.json})
rather than from any configuration the run superseded. Twelve models,
seven families, at most two per family, selected to control the
response-length artifact: median response length spans 486--1413
characters ($2.91\times$), and the length--accuracy correlation inside
the roster is $r = +0.286$ against $+0.597$ across all 36 valid models.

Four properties of this roster bound what its results may claim.
\texttt{gemini-3.1-pro\_5-shots} is a five-shot prompting condition in
an otherwise matched roster and is labelled as such wherever it
appears. \texttt{iask\_pro} and \texttt{arx\_3} are systems of
unknown provenance --- plausibly retrieval-augmented or ensembles ---
so rows involving them support arena claims but not claims about base
LLM capability. \texttt{Meta-Llama-3-8B} is a base (non-instruct)
model. And \texttt{claude-3-5-sonnet-20241022} /
\texttt{claude-3.5-sonnet} are two harnesses of nominally the same
model that agree on only $83.4\%$ of predictions; they were included
as a pre-registered resolution check. Its outcome: the scheduler never
paired them directly across 2{,}640 comparisons, leaving two of the
three registered predictions unevaluable, and they finish at ranks 4
and 8 of a mid-table whose five scores span $0.957$--$1.091$ --- a
separation well inside the corrected standard errors.

\section{The Exclusion Policy}

Ten models are excluded at roster load by a hard-coded list that
raises, each entry carrying its measured reason. The list covers one
identifier-space mismatch, six archives with no stored reasoning text,
and three mixed-format models that emit a bare answer on $68.4\%$,
$35.4\%$ and $27.6\%$ of items respectively. The operative threshold is
format \emph{consistency}, not text presence: a model that answers
bare on a third of items reintroduces the format preference inside its
own comparisons.

Three notes belong with the policy.

\textbf{One name is not the other.} \texttt{Meta-Llama-3\_1-70B-Instruct}
is in the roster and is clean. \texttt{Meta-Llama-3-70B-Instruct} is
excluded, sits in a different question-identifier space, and overlaps
the other models' law questions on exactly one item; its presence in an
intersection is what collapsed law from 287 questions to 20 and
inverted a domain recommendation (Section~\ref{sec:res-law}).

\textbf{The policy does not generalise.} Six of the ten exclusions are
for exactly the condition the corpus validator's trace-presence check
measures and declines to act on, because that check has no failure
branch. The policy is a ratchet on known failures, not a threshold, and
a new model that shares the defect would be admitted. The general guard
is a one-line change and is named as future work
(Section~\ref{sec:disc-future}).

\textbf{The judge is in no roster.} The judge model appears in none of
the three rosters, so there is no self-preference confound in any
reported run. No constraint enforces this; it is a property of these
runs.
