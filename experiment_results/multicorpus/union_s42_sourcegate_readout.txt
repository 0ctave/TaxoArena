Reading. sourceGate is a NULL: it changes 8 of 6,482 verdicts and none of the per-source rates by more than 0.2pp. The
reason is structural — the production descent's first step from the union root is already, in effect, an argmax over the
six corpus-root directions, so hard-gating on the same directions reproduces it. Its source flow is the stage-1b flow
question for question (MMLU-Pro -> GPQA 323 vs 324, -> MATH 250 vs 251), which confirms that the 22% of MMLU-Pro
questions the union gives away are lost at the ROOT DIRECTIONS, not at the beam width or the descent gate: a per-corpus
root vMF fitted on a whole corpus is simply too coarse to separate MMLU-Pro from its neighbours, whatever rule reads it.
That is the negative result that makes flatLeaf the fix: the discriminative information lives in the leaf centroids.
