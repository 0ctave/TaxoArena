Reading. flatLeaf recovers the whole root-descent loss: 81.58% is exactly the flat nearest-centroid sweep stage 1b read
over the same leaves (0.81580), so the sweep was not an unreachable upper bound but a routing rule the union can adopt.
The corpus that the six-way root step was giving away — MMLU-Pro — gains the most: source accuracy 78.00% -> 96.18% and
Top-1 59.46% -> 74.06%, i.e. within 1.7pp of the 75.78% the MMLU-Pro tree scores when routed alone; BBH goes to a clean
100%. The cost lands on the two small corpora that the root descent had been over-feeding: GPQA 87.99% -> 77.65% and
MATH 65.58% -> 60.80%, both losing questions to MMLU-Pro leaves that are genuinely closer (GPQA physics/chemistry,
MATH word problems). That is the same semantic adjacency stage 1b described, now resolved in MMLU-Pro's favour because
the leaf centroid, not a six-way root softmax with a beam/descent gate, decides. Net: +616 questions.
