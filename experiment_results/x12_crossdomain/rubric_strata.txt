  READING: the card acts ONLY where the reference is already right. Where the reference fails - the
  stratum where the judge sits at 0.35-0.39 and most needs help - it contributes exactly nothing, on
  both judge families, with identical 6:6 discordance. A rubric is instructions for reasoning, and
  FORMAT-REF showed the judge's own reasoning is largely switched off once a reference is present.
  Where the reference is wrong the judge transcribes something wrong, and instructions have nothing
  to act on. A rubric cannot substitute for a reference; it can only improve the use of a working one.
