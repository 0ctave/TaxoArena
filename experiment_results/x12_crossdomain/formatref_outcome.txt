  (3) NEGATIVE CONTROL: with no reference in the prompt the REF coefficient is +0.507 +- 0.111 (z=+4.6).
      OUTSIDE the registered +-0.2 band. The REF contrast picks up signal even when the judge never
      saw a reference, so it is partly a proxy for question difficulty or for consensus among
      models, and the CONSENSUS-FIT coefficient is an upper bound rather than a clean estimate.

  DIAGNOSTIC: the two contrasts are correlated, because the reference is right most of the time.
      (a) PAIRWISE grok, with reference                    corr(KEY, REF) 0.770 | agree on 0.896 of matches
      (b) POINTWISE grok, with reference                   corr(KEY, REF) 0.769 | agree on 0.896 of matches
      (c) POINTWISE grok, NO reference (negative control)  corr(KEY, REF) 0.769 | agree on 0.896 of matches
      (d) PAIRWISE Mistral, with reference                 corr(KEY, REF) 0.770 | agree on 0.896 of matches
      Where KEY lands near zero the RATIO explodes and its bootstrap interval is meaningless; the
      paired contrasts below therefore compare the COEFFICIENTS and their difference REF-KEY, which
      stay well behaved. This supersedes the ratio as the registered statistic - the registration
      named a quantity that turns out to be undefined in one of the four arms.
