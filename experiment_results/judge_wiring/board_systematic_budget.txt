=== SYSTEMATIC-BUDGET: fit the continuous widener, and check it independently ===
  form: tau(A) = exp(b0 + b1*A) added in quadrature to the bootstrap SD; call iff
  min(c_raw, Phi(|m|/sqrt(s^2+tau^2))) >= 0.975. TWO free parameters, NO key at inference.

  THE POPULATION, and the correction it forces to a number already published:
    edges with ANY key gap (the honest denominator): 918, of which 57 point the wrong way
    edges with a key gap >= 0.02 (what SYSTEMATIC-SIGNAL reported): 890, 42 wrong
    raw certificate on ALL edges:      745 called, 7 wrong = 0.94%
    raw certificate on FILTERED edges: 739 called, 6 wrong = 0.81%
    The key-gap filter is not available at deployment AND it selects the population - the
    excluded edges are far more often wrong. Every published rate must be restated on the
    unfiltered denominator.

=== (4) LABEL NOISE: how much of the fit is edges the key cannot actually adjudicate? ===
  An edge's 'wrong direction' label rests on a key gap that is itself an estimate. Refit using
  only edges whose key direction is established by an exact binomial at 5%.
  test: exact two-sided McNemar on the two models' per-question correctness in that anchor.
  established at 5%: 810 of 890 edges | indeterminate: 80
  wrong-direction edges: 25 of 810 sit on ESTABLISHED labels, 17 of 80 on indeterminate ones
  so 17 of the 42 wrong edges (40%) rest on a key direction the key does not establish
  refit on established-direction edges only: b1 = +2.0451 against +3.0576 (67% of it)
  -> the fitted effect SURVIVES the attack that killed the conformal form

=== (5) THE COMPARISON THAT DECIDES WHETHER THE FIT EARNS ITS KEEP ===
  SYSTEMATIC-SIGNAL rejected a hard gate on the grounds that at a ~1% base rate any gate
  catching half the errors discards many good calls. That argued against a gate as a SUBSTITUTE
  for the certificate. It did NOT establish that a fitted two-parameter widener beats one. This
  compares them on the same edges at the same operating point, out of fold for the budget.
