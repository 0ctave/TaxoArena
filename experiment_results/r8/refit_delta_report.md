The refit's GT-agreement column also reproduces the published overallSpearmanRho per domain (small deviations reflect the GT reference: this script scores accuracy over ALL reserved questions in the category, the in-app metric over the benchmark's judged query set). The contamination is confined to per-leaf fits (table above) and to the run-time stopping decisions those fits fed, which no post-hoc refit can repair: the corrected estimator runs on the design the biased estimator sampled.
